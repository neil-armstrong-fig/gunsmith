Privacy Policy

You can play Gunsmith without an account. Google sign-in lets you make weapons and arenas with AI and save them to your account.

Who runs Gunsmith

Neil Armstrong operates Gunsmith from Northern Ireland and is the data controller for the game. Privacy questions and requests can be sent through Neil’s contact form.

Playing and signing in

  • Practice and browser-local Make. Your preferences, remembered choices and browser-local shelf stay on your device. Guest Make uses built-in items on your device.
  • Private rooms. The server processes your room and seat information, gameplay inputs and match state to run the match and reconnect you to your seat. If you choose an account item for a room match, the other player can see the chosen weapon or arena details available in that room. Your Make prompt stays private from the other player.
  • Google sign-in. Gunsmith requests only Google’s openid scope. It uses the stable Google account identifier to recognise you and keeps its own account ID, generated display name, sign-up date and hashed session records. It does not request your Google email address, profile or contacts.
  • Credits. The account service keeps a balance and credit records to apply the daily allowance and limit abuse.

Make, prompts and your shelf

When a signed-in Make request reaches the AI provider, Gunsmith sends your prompt to Anthropic to check it and create the requested weapon or arena. Gunsmith does not add your Google identifier or account ID to the provider request. Keep names, contact details and other personal information out of prompts.

When Make creates an item, Gunsmith stores the result and the original prompt under your account before you click Save. This lets you add it to your shelf and reuse the prompt. Removing an item from the shelf frees a place and leaves the item and prompt in account storage. Separate prompt history for internal tuning is currently disabled.

Why Gunsmith uses this information

Gunsmith processes room, account and Make data to provide the features you ask to use. It relies on legitimate interests to secure the service, limit abuse and troubleshoot faults. It also processes information where required by law.

You can object to processing that relies on legitimate interests. Send an objection through Neil’s contact form.

Cookies and browser storage

Google sign-in uses a secure sign-in cookie that lasts up to ten minutes. Signing in creates a secure session cookie that lasts up to 30 days. Both cookies use HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax.

Local storage keeps your preferences, remembered choices and browser-local shelf until you clear site data. Session storage keeps a private-room reconnect token in your tab. These are functional uses. Gunsmith does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Who processes information

Cloudflare hosts the game, API and database and may process connection details such as your IP address to deliver and protect the service. Google handles sign-in. Anthropic processes prompts and model inputs and outputs for signed-in Make under its commercial terms.

Providers may process information outside the UK. Where UK data protection law requires a safeguard for a transfer, the applicable provider terms and transfer safeguards govern it. Use the contact form to ask about those safeguards or request a copy.

How long information is kept

Account information, generated items and their prompts are kept until account deletion. Expired hashed sessions cannot be used. Private-room state is kept for running the room and allowing reconnection. Providers may retain logs or backups for additional periods under their own terms.

Deleting your account removes the live account, sessions, generated items and prompts. For 90 days, Gunsmith keeps a protected digest of your Google account identifier, a link to the credit ledger and the remaining credit records so deleting and signing in again cannot reset the allowance. The next successful cleanup after the 90 days removes those records. You can clear browser-local data in your browser.

Your choices and rights

Use Neil’s contact form to request account deletion or to ask for access, correction, erasure, restriction or a copy of your information. Include “Gunsmith privacy” and describe the request. Gunsmith may need to verify your identity before releasing or deleting account information. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

If you are a child and want help with sign-in, prompts or a privacy request, ask a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian can also use the contact form.
